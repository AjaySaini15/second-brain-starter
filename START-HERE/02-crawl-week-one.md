# Crawl — week one

**Goal:** make the AI useful daily with just a chat tool. 15 minutes a day, no new software.

## The daily loop
1. Bring it one real piece of work every day — notes to structure, an email to draft, a decision to think through. Real work only; toy prompts teach you nothing.
2. Every wrong output → one new line in CONTEXT.md. (Names it didn't know, preferences it violated, context it lacked.)
3. Re-paste the updated CONTEXT.md into your project/instructions at the end of the week.

## Add your second skill
When one loop feels natural, add `skills/weekly-review.md` and run it Friday on the week's notes. Ten minutes in, you'll have the clearest picture of your week you've had in years.

## What to expect (honestly)
This week costs you MORE time than it saves. That's normal — you're doing reps. It's a workout: muscles hurt first, results show later. The people who quit in week one are the ones who expected a vending machine.

## By Sunday you should have
- A CONTEXT.md that's grown 2× since Saturday
- Two skills you've actually run
- A feel for the correction habit

**Then:** `03-walk-first-month.md` — where the AI stops being a chat window and starts having eyes.
